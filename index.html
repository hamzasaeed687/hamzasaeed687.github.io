PERFORMANCELOAD / STRESS TESTING

Controlled Locust stress assessment

Locust was used to simulate concurrent users and observe response latency, throughput and failure behavior. The results show degradation under concurrency rather than a simple steady increase in latency.

200users
11%failures
23.4savg latency
81.7smax latency
BOTTLENECK ANALYSIS

At 200 users, the median response is already 20 seconds and the 95th percentile reaches 81 seconds, with 30 failures out of 274 requests. This is consistent with backend saturation, slow dependency/database work, queueing or timeout pressure.

SATURATIONSTRESS TEST · 500 USERS

Capacity limit observed under heavier concurrency

The 500-user run produced 23,408 requests with a 100% failure rate. The median was only 34 ms while the average reached 1.41 seconds and the maximum reached 225.89 seconds, indicating a highly skewed response distribution and severe failure behavior under load.

500users
100%failures
71.8RPS
225.9smax latency
WHAT THE RESULT SUGGESTS

The low median alongside a much larger average and extreme maximum suggests many requests fail quickly while a smaller subset becomes severely delayed. The next diagnostic step would be correlation with server CPU/memory, database latency, connection pools, upstream dependencies and timeout/error logs.
